# arXiv Hop privacy policy

arXiv Hop reads citation and bibliography text from arXiv HTML papers in your browser so it can resolve links and show previews. It has no developer-operated backend, account sign-in, analytics, or advertising.

When you hover over an unresolved citation with a usable title, the extension may send words from that published title to the arXiv public API to look for a match. It caches recent results in your browser for up to a day. If you choose a Google search fallback, your browser opens Google Search with the citation query. Destination sites process requests under their own privacy policies.

When you click **Download PDF** on an abstract page or the download icon in an HTML paper, the extension downloads the PDF from arXiv. If you select a folder, it uses access granted by your browser to save PDFs there and check for existing copies. Preferences and folder access are stored locally in your browser. You can reset the folder from the extension or clear its stored data by removing the extension.

arXiv Hop handles this data in accordance with the [Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use).

For privacy questions, use the [arXiv Hop issue tracker](https://github.com/JeffryCA/arxiv-hop/issues).
